2. What Tirelo is

Tirelo is a personal budgeting notebook. You enter or import your own transactions, and the app shows overviews, recurring costs, budgets, forecasts and plans. Tirelo does not connect to your bank, cannot see or move money, and does not provide banking, payment, credit, investment or insurance services.

3. No advice

Everything the app shows, including categories, budgets, forecasts, debt plans and their suggested order, net worth, and in the web version the answers from Claude, is information to help you think. It is not financial, tax, legal or investment advice. You make your own decisions; for important ones, ask a qualified professional.

4. Your figures and their accuracy

The app is only as accurate as what you enter or import. Reading bank files (CSV or PDF) and sorting payments automatically can go wrong, and banks change their file formats. Check important amounts against your bank statements. Forecasts, budgets and debt plans are estimates, not promises.

5. Your data and backups

  • Make backups regularly (Settings → Your data), and before you change phones.
  • If you forget the PIN of the app lock, the only way back in is to delete the data on the device. If you forget the password of a protected backup, nobody can open it, the developer included.

How data is handled is described in the privacy policy.

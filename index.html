Tirelo privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: Tirelo keeps your figures on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet. The developer never receives your figures. The only thing that can reach the developer is an e-mail you decide to send yourself.

Who is responsible

Tirelo is made by Bart in Belgium. Questions about this policy: flexiq.mobile@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

The transactions, recurring payments, budgets, goals, debts, settings and notes you enter or import are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Your phone may include them in its own backup (iCloud or Google), depending on your phone’s settings.

Bank files you import

When you import a CSV file or a PDF statement from your bank, the app reads it on your device. Nothing from the file is sent anywhere, and the developer never sees it.

Exports and backups

A CSV export or a backup file is made only when you ask for it. It opens your phone’s share sheet, and you choose where it goes, for example the Files app, an e-mail or a cloud drive. From then on, the privacy terms of that destination apply.

Security

You can turn on an app lock with a PIN. Only a salted hash of the PIN is stored on your device, never the PIN itself, and it is not part of any backup. A backup can be protected with a password: it is then encrypted on your device (AES-256) before it leaves the app, and cannot be opened without that password. The app cannot load anything from the internet.

What the developer collects

Nothing automatically. The app does not collect, send, sell or share personal data, and it contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. The only exception is an e-mail you send yourself, described below.

Feedback and problem reports

Settings → Feedback opens your own e-mail app with a message filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it from there. If you do, the developer receives:

  • your e-mail address, and your name if your e-mail app adds it;
  • what you wrote;
  • for a problem report, if you leave “Add technical details” on: the app version, the type of device and system, the language and look you use, the screen size, how many months, payments, recurring costs, accounts and categories the app holds, and the last few error messages, without any text they quoted. These details are shown to you before you send them. They never include names, notes or amounts.

The developer uses them only to reply to you, to fix problems and to improve the app, because you asked for it and because the developer has a legitimate interest in supporting the app (GDPR, article 6(1)(b) and (f)). Messages are kept as long as needed to handle them, and deleted at the latest 12 months after the last message about the subject. They are not shared with anyone, apart from the e-mail provider that delivers and stores them for the developer (Google, through Gmail).

Children

The app is meant for adults managing household money and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data in the app, or uninstall the app. Both remove the figures from your device. Copies you exported yourself are not affected.

Your rights

The developer holds no personal data about you, except e-mails you sent. For those you can ask to see, correct or delete them, ask for a copy, or object to their use, by writing to the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be / autoriteprotectiondonnees.be).

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: flexiq.mobile@gmail.com. Please mention your phone model.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, for example to sync between phones, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change. The terms of use explain what the app is and isn’t.
